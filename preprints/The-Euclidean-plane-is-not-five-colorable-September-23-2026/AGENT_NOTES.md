- [Shisheng Li and Yong Su, *Every n points in the plane contain (m1 + o(1)) n points with no unit distance*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15424) (arXiv:2610.15424, 11 Oct 2026). Proves f(n)/n → m1(R^2), identifying the limiting worst-case finite independence ratio with the maximal measurable unit-distance-avoiding density; the abstract states that its "only non-classical input is the Haar rigidity theorem for wild character laws" from this preprint. - [Ákos Dúcz (contributor), *Measurable Independence Density Equals the Finite Independence-Ratio Infimum in the Euclidean Plane*](https://hexagonmath.org/abs/2610.00171v1) (hexagon:2610.00171v1, 9 Oct 2026). Claims that m1(R^2) equals the infimum of independence ratios of finite unit-distance graphs, using this paper's spectral rigidity theorem as a black box and following its weak-measurable reduction. - [Henry Bass, *euclidean-chromatic-transfer* (GitHub repository with five preprints)](https://github.com/outsidetext/euclidean-chromatic-transfer) (GitHub, Oct 2026). Extends this paper's transfer theorem to R^n, claiming among other things χ(R^3) ≥ 7 and lower bounds for planes with two forbidden distances, with a Lean 4 project reusing modules from openai/math (the README says some numerical density bounds enter as explicit hypotheses); the acknowledgements state that results, proofs and formalization were developed with help from Claude (Anthropic).
- [Mikhail Koshelev, Andrey Kupavskii, Vsevolod Voronov, *Ordinary and measurable colorings of Euclidean three-space*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18907) (arXiv:2610.18907, 11 Oct 2026). Proves that every finite proper coloring of R^3 can be replaced by a proper Lebesgue-measurable coloring with the same number of colors, giving 7 ≤ χ(R^3) = χ_m(R^3) ≤ 15; Theorem 2.3 of this paper is assumed as an external input ("We do not prove it here") and its transfer method is adapted.
- [Carson Crocker, *A conditional transfer for colourings of R^n, with the conditional consequence χ(R^3) ≥ 7*](https://zenodo.org/records/23633577) (Zenodo, version 2.0, 24 Oct 2026). Conditionally on Theorem 2.3 of this paper, extends the transfer to R^n and to sets of positive real algebraic distances, giving χ(R^n) ≥ ⌈1/m₁(R^n)⌉; version 2 adds a remark that α₁(R²) = m₁(R²) implies f(n)/n = m₁(R²) + o(1). The record states that it was written with AI assistance (Claude), was checked by AI referee passes, and has not been reviewed by a human expert. [AI-generated per source]
- [Jonathan Davidson, *Coloring Monomial Cayley Graphs: The One-Parameter Case*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09474) (arXiv:2610.09474, 7 Oct 2026). Studies chromatic numbers of Cayley graphs on unit steps given by signed powers of one complex parameter; its introduction combines this paper's reported bound with de Bruijn–Erdős compactness and notes of the resulting finite obstruction that "the argument does not identify such a parameter set or bound its size."
- [Sergi Galán, *The ruler-and-compass plane is not 5-colourable*](https://github.com/decalion89/chromatic-number-of-the-plane/blob/main/notes/six_over_fields.md) (research note, 9 Oct 2026).
