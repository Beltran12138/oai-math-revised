- [Nir Ailon, *Tighter Fourier Transform Complexity Tradeoffs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.1741) (arXiv:1404.1741, 2014–2015). Shows that speeding up the FFT by a factor b forces Ω(n) ill-conditioned intermediate steps, which bears on the paper's statement that it makes no conditioning or numerical-stability claim (the bibliography cites Ailon's arXiv:1305.4745 and arXiv:1403.1307, not this one).
- [Christos H. Papadimitriou, *Optimality of the fast Fourier transform*](https://projects.csail.mit.edu/jacm/jacm79.html) (J. ACM 26(1):95–102, 1979). A classical optimality result for FFT addition counts under assumptions on the algorithm's graph; relevant to the introduction's point that n log n lower bounds depend on the permitted operations.
- [Pavel Pudlák, *A Note On the Use of Determinant for Proving Lower Bounds on the Size of Linear Circuits*](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/1998/042) (ECCC TR98-042, 1998). Gives determinant-based lower bounds for bounded-coefficient linear circuits, extending the Morgenstern-type bounds that the paper says its unrestricted-coefficient model sidesteps.
- [Adelmo Morrison Orozco, Nathan Sheffield and Ryan Williams, *Computing faster with a full memory: Catalytic algorithms break time-space lower bounds*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24377) (arXiv:2610.24377, 2026-10-17). Gives a catalytic DFT over suitable rings for $n$ a power of 2 in near-linear time with $O(\log n)$ clean space, related to the paper's remark that its replay lemma restores borrowed storage as in catalytic space (the bibliography cites only Buhrman et al. 2014 on this theme); the arXiv paper does not cite this release.
