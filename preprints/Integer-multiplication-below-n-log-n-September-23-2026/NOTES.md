- [David Harvey, *On the recently proposed o(n log n) integer multiplication algorithm*](https://web.maths.unsw.edu.au/~davidharvey/research/subnlogn.pdf) (note, 2026-10-20). An exposition by a co-author of the 2021 O(n log n) algorithm that the paper builds on: it explains the core gadget and the tensor amplification step by step, calls the main result plausible, and lists the parts the author has not yet checked (the interaction with synthetic roots of unity, the rest of the 2021 algorithm with growing parameters, and data rearrangement costs in the Turing model).
