An earlier non-perturbative claim of the same implication, withdrawn because its momentum-space OPE step fails; this is relevant to the paper's discussion of nonperturbative arguments and of DFKLP2016.
- [F. Ceyhan and T. Faulkner, *Recovering the QNEC from the ANEC*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.04683) (arXiv:1812.04683, 2018). Uses half-sided modular inclusions for null-cut algebras; this is related to the paper's step 1, which builds positive null-cut generators from half-sided modular inclusions.
- [V. Morinelli, Y. Tanimoto and B. Wegener, *Modular operator for null plane algebras in free fields*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.00039) (arXiv:2107.00039, 2021). Decomposes the modular operator of null-cut algebras into lightlike fibres for the free scalar; this is related to the paper's bounded-cut modular Ward formula and its free-scalar example.
- [H. Bostelmann, *Phase space properties and the short distance structure in quantum field theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0409070) (arXiv:math-ph/0409070; J. Math. Phys. 46 (2005) 052301). Recovers pointlike fields from a net of local algebras under a phase-space condition; this is related to the paper's reconstruction of the improvement scalar L as a field affiliated with the bounded net, which explicitly avoids phase-space assumptions.
