Shows that some reparametrizations of the zero section of $T^*S^n$, viewed as maps, are not Hamiltonian isotopic to the original zero section; this is an earlier non-isotopy phenomenon to compare with Theorem 1.1, which concerns the embedded submanifold $L$.

Constrains the smooth type of exact Lagrangians in $T^*S^n$; related to the paper's point that the obstruction lies in how $L$ sits in $T^*Q$, not in its abstract smooth structure, which agrees with $Q$.

Proves a Hamiltonian-isotopy uniqueness statement of nearby-Lagrangian type for singular Lagrangian skeleta in rational homology balls; this is a recent positive case to read alongside the low-dimensional results surveyed in the introduction.
