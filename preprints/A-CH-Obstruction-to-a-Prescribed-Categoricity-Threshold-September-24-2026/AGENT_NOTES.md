Assuming cardinal exponentiation is injective, it shows that for AECs with amalgamation and arbitrarily large models the categoricity spectrum is empty, a closed interval of finite successors of LS(K), or an end segment starting below the Hanf number; this is relevant because the paper's CH example has neither amalgamation nor joint embedding.

Shows that a strongly compact cardinal bounds Hanf numbers for amalgamation and related properties, using a functorial relational presentation theorem; this connects to the paper's use of the Hanf bound $H(K)$ and its discussion of where amalgamation and joint embedding fail.
