- [P. Goerss, H.-W. Henn, M. Mahowald, C. Rezk, *A resolution of the K(2)-local sphere at the prime 3*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.2175) (arXiv:0706.2175; Ann. of Math. 162 (2005)). Builds a finite resolution of the K(2)-local sphere at p=3, a height-two case beyond the p≥5 finiteness results cited in the paper's history section.
- [A. Beaudry, P. G. Goerss, H.-W. Henn, *Chromatic splitting for the K(2)-local sphere at p=2*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.08182) (arXiv:1712.08182; Geom. Topol. 26 (2022)). Computes continuous cohomology of the height-two stabilizer group with Lubin–Tate coefficients E_0 at p=2, the kind of coefficient cohomology whose finiteness (mod p) is the paper's main algebraic input.
- [T. Barthel, T. M. Schlank, N. Stapleton, J. Weinstein, *On Hopkins' Picard group*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.20958) (arXiv:2407.20958, v2 December 2025). Uses p-adic geometry (Drinfeld's symmetric space) to compute the algebraic Picard group of K(n)-local spectra at all heights and primes, bearing on the paper's remark that degreewise finite generation does not extend to arbitrary invertible K(n)-local spectra.
