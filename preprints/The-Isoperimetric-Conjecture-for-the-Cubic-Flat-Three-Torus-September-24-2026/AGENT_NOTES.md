## Possibly relevant literature (found by the agent, not checked)
- [F. Glaudo, *On the Isoperimetric Profile of the Hypercube*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.20051) (arXiv:2305.20051, May 2023). Shows that subsets of the cube (0,1)^d with volume sufficiently close to 1/2 have relative perimeter at least 1; relates to the paper's discussion of near-half-volume results, which cites only Acerbi–Fusco–Morini, Hadwiger and Barthe–Maurey.
- [L. Yu, *An explicit dimension-free isoperimetric plateau in the cube*](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.18351) (arXiv:2610.18351, October 2026). Proves that in every dimension the relative profile of the unit cube equals one, with coordinate slabs as the only minimizers, for volumes within 6.25×10⁻¹² of 1/2; relates to the paper's remark that earlier near-half-volume results came without effective bounds.
- [L. Heller, S. Heller, M. Traizet, *The Enclosed Volume for Periodic Constant Mean Curvature Surfaces*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14935) (arXiv:2601.14935, January 2026). Gives a formula for the enclosed volume of lattice-periodic CMC surfaces and a counterexample concerning the isoperimetric problem in T²×ℝ; context for the periodic isoperimetric problem of Hauswirth–Pérez–Romon–Ros that the paper builds on.
- [Emanuel Milman, *Spectral and Isoperimetric Bounds on Flat Tori*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13052) (arXiv:2608.13052, 2026-08-13). Its bounds for isoperimetric profiles and Cheeger constants on general flat tori provide context for the manuscript's claimed exact profile on the unit cubic three-torus.
- [Ben Baker, Joseph Briggs, Manuel Fernandez V and Chris Wells, *Isoperimetric Inequalities in the $\ell_{\infty}$ cube and torus*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22456) (arXiv:2609.22456, 2026-09-18). Its exact isoperimetric inequality for cubes and tori in the $\ell_{\infty}$ metric provides a different-metric analogue of the manuscript's claimed Euclidean cubic-torus profile.
