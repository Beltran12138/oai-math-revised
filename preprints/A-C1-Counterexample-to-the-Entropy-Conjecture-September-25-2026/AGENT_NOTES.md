## Possibly relevant literature (found by the agent, not checked)
- [D. Fried and M. Shub, *Entropy, linearity and chain-recurrence*](http://www.numdam.org/item?id=PMIHES_1979__50__203_0) (Publ. Math. IHÉS 50, 1979, pp. 203–214). Classic paper on the entropy conjecture and its low-regularity failures; background for the paper's statement that earlier counterexamples were Lipschitz and the $C^1$ question was distinct.
- [I. Kangasniemi, Y. Okuyama, P. Pankka, T. Sahlsten, *Entropy in uniformly quasiregular dynamics*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.10183) (arXiv:1903.10183, 2019). Proves Shub's entropy conjecture for non-injective uniformly quasiregular self-maps ($h(f)=\log\deg f$), a positive result for a class of noninvertible maps to compare with the paper's claim that noninvertibility escapes known positive results.
- [S. Sandfeldt, *Bounds of the spectral radius of the induced map on cohomology*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.07572) (arXiv:2106.07572, 2021). For $C^1$ diffeomorphisms with spectral radius $>1$ on cohomology it finds an ergodic measure with a positive Lyapunov exponent; relevant to the paper's remark that its counterexample is noninvertible and does not address $C^1$ diffeomorphisms.
