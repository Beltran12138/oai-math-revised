Shows that weak Zariski decompositions give minimal models for pseudo-effective generalized klt pairs, and that flips terminate for generalized lc pairs with weak Zariski decompositions (assuming termination in lower dimensions); this is the same weak-Zariski-decomposition route to existence that Theorem 1.1 takes.
