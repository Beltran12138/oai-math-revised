## Possibly relevant literature (found by the agent, not checked)
- [Le Kuai, Kefeng Liu, Yunhui Wu, *Rigidity of complex bounded domains with finite-volume quotients*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.00459) (arXiv:1801.00459, v1 2018, v3 2 Oct 2026). Proves that bounded convex domains with finite-volume quotients are bounded symmetric and addresses Kazhdan's conjecture, which sits next to the paper's discussion of Frankel's convex compact-quotient theorem.
- [Alan M. Nadel, *Semisimplicity of the group of biholomorphisms of the universal covering of a compact complex manifold with ample canonical bundle*](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/1990/132-2/p01) (Ann. of Math. 132 (1990), 193–211). Concerns automorphism groups of domains whose compact quotients have ample canonical bundle, which is the situation the introduction recalls from Kollár–Pardon §3.
- [A. V. Isaev and S. G. Krantz, *Domains with non-compact automorphism group: a survey*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9612201) (arXiv:math/9612201, 1997). Surveys the Wong–Rosay ball characterisation and boundary orbit-accumulation methods, which the paper's first step extends to singular affine ambient spaces.
- [Andrew M. Zimmer, *Rigidity of complex convex divisible sets*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.4116) (arXiv:1308.4116, 2013). An earlier rigidity theorem for complex convex sets with cocompact automorphism groups; the paper itself cites only Zimmer's later C^{1,1}-boundary result.
- [Vasily Rogov, *On the metric Kollár-Pardon problem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.07983) (arXiv:2401.07983, 2024-01-15). Its homogeneity theorem for aspherical universal covers with definable lifted metrics offers a comparison for the paper's passage from semialgebraicity and compact quotient to symmetry, under additional metric hypotheses.
