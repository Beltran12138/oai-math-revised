- [C. H. Taubes, *Tamed to compatible: Symplectic forms via moduli space integration*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.5440) (arXiv:0910.5440, 2009). For compact four-manifolds with $b^+=1$, it produces compatible symplectic forms cohomologous to a given taming form for an open dense set of tamed almost-complex structures, using currents from pseudoholomorphic curves; this bears on the paper's tamed-to-compatible and prescribed-taming-class steps, which work with $b^+=3$.
- [Q. Tan, H. Wang, J. Zhou, P. Zhu, *On tamed almost complex four manifolds*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.02948) (arXiv:1712.02948, 2017). It proves existence of a compatible symplectic form on tamed closed almost-complex four-manifolds under a condition on $h^-_J$ and $b^+$, earlier partial work on the tamed-to-compatible question the paper uses in Sections 3–4.
- [K. Zheng, *The space of closed G₂-structures. II. Concavity and hypersymplectic structures*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.20456) (arXiv:2507.20456, 2025). It studies concavity of Hitchin's volume functional along geodesics of closed G₂-structures and constructs hypersymplectic examples, adding to the flow and G₂-reduction literature surveyed in the paper's introduction.
