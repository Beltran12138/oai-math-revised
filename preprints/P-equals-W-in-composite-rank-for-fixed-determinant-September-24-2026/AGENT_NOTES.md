- [Anne Larsen, *Fourier duality on compactified Prym fibrations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31604) (arXiv:2609.31604, 25 September 2026). Proves multiplicativity of the perverse filtration for compactified Prym fibrations, including SL Higgs moduli over the elliptic locus, extending the Maulik–Shen–Yin Fourier framework that the paper cites as [MSY2025].
- [Michael Groechenig, Dimitri Wyss, Paul Ziegler, *Mirror symmetry for moduli spaces of Higgs bundles via p-adic integration*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06417) (arXiv:1707.06417, 2017). Proves the Hausel–Thaddeus topological mirror symmetry conjecture for smooth SL_n/PGL_n Higgs moduli in all ranks; this concerns the variant cohomology, which Theorem 1.1 newly covers in composite rank.
- [Camilla Felisetti, Mirko Mauri, *P=W conjectures for character varieties with symplectic resolution*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08752) (arXiv:2006.08752, 2020). Proves P=W and PI=WI for GL_n and SL_n character varieties that admit symplectic resolutions; this is a non-coprime companion to the paper's coprime fixed-determinant statement.

Section 2 reviews SLₙ endoscopic decomposition, including the canonical twist, providing background for the manuscript's extension of the filtration comparison to variant cohomology in composite rank.
