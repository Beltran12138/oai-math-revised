- [N. Feld, *Finite-coefficient K-theory of henselian valued fields and Gersten injectivity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18789) (arXiv:2608.18789, August 2026). Proves finite-coefficient Gersten injectivity for noetherian henselian regular local rings with invertible coefficients; a point of comparison for the paper's mod-5 generic noninjectivity, where 5 is the residue characteristic.
