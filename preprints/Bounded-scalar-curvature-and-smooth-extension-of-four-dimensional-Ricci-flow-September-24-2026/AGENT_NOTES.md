## Possibly relevant literature (found by the agent, not checked)
- [Max Hallgren, *Ricci Flow with Ricci Curvature and Volume Bounded Below*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.03386) (arXiv:2104.03386, 2021). For simply connected closed four-dimensional flows with Ricci curvature and volume bounded below, finite-time singularities are $C^0$ orbifolds and the flow continues by orbifold Ricci flow; a related four-dimensional continuation result, compared with the paper's claim of smooth continuation on $M$.
- [Wangjian Jian, *Global $\varepsilon$-regularity for 4-dimensional Ricci flow with integral scalar curvature bound*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.06881) (arXiv:2008.06881; Pacific J. Math. 319 (2022), 333–342). Extends the Ge–Jiang global $\varepsilon$-regularity for four-dimensional flows with bounded scalar curvature; related to the paper's concentration and scale analysis in Section 4.
- [Reto Buzano and Gianmichele Di Matteo, *A Local Singularity Analysis for the Ricci Flow and its Applications to Ricci Flows with Bounded Scalar Curvature*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.16227) (arXiv:2006.16227; Calc. Var. PDE 61 (2022), 65). Part I of the local singularity analysis; the paper cites only Part II (arXiv:2606.12379) for scalar blowup at local Type I points.
- [Shota Hamanaka, *Type of finite time singularities of the Ricci flow with bounded scalar curvature*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.08250) (arXiv:2105.08250, 2021). Studies blow-ups at locally Type I singularities of closed flows with bounded scalar curvature in dimensions 4 to 7; related to the special cases surveyed in the paper's introduction.
- [Xianbin Huang and Wancheng Zhang, *$L^2$ Curvature Bounds on Ricci flows with bounded scalar curvature up to dimension 7*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10575) (arXiv:2610.10575, 2026-10-05). Extends the four-dimensional $L^2$ curvature and $L^4$ Ricci bounds of Bamler–Zhang, which the paper uses, to dimensions $n\le 7$.
