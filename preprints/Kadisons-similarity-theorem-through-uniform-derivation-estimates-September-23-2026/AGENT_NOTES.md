## Possibly relevant literature (found by the agent, not checked)
- [E. Papapetros, *A new approach to the similarity problem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.19668) (arXiv:2403.19668, 2024; published in [Advances in Operator Theory 9, article 65](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43036-024-00363-4), 2 July 2024). Shows that the hypothesis "every separably acting von Neumann algebra with a cyclic vector is hyperreflexive" implies the similarity property for all C*-algebras, a weaker form of the paper's Corollary 1.3 (universal hyperreflexivity).
- [G. K. Eleftherakis and E. Papapetros, *The similarity problem and hyperreflexivity of von Neumann algebras*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.07605) (arXiv:2306.07605, 2023; revised 17 January 2024; listed as forthcoming in J. Operator Theory). Characterizes the weak similarity property of a von Neumann algebra by hyperreflexivity of tensor products of its commutant with B(ℓ²(I)), which bears on the hyperreflexivity consequence in Corollary 1.3.
- [J. Cameron, E. Christensen, A. M. Sinclair, R. R. Smith, S. White and A. D. Wiggins, *A remark on the similarity and perturbation problems*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.5405) (arXiv:1206.5405; C. R. Acad. Sci. Canada 35(2), 2013). Shows that Kadison's similarity problem is equivalent to whether close C*-algebras must have close commutants, so Theorem 1.1 would also have consequences for that perturbation question.
- [G. Pisier, *A note on strong similarity and the Connes embedding problem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10654) (arXiv:2601.10654, Jan–Feb 2026). Constructs a completely bounded homomorphism into a QWEP von Neumann algebra that is not strongly similar to a $*$-homomorphism, which is background on where the similarity $S$ (and the operators implementing derivations via Paulsen's theorem) can be taken to lie.
