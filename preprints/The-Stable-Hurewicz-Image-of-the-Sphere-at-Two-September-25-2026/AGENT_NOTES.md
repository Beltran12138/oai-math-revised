- [Hadi Zare, *On the Bott periodicity, J-homomorphisms, transfer maps and H_*Q_0S^{-n}*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.4478) (arXiv:0911.4478, 2009–2010). Shows that Curtis's conjecture holds when restricted to the factor J in Sullivan's splitting Q_0S^0 = J × cok J, a partial case of Theorem 1.1.
- [Hadi Zare, *On the Hurewicz homomorphism on the extensions of ideals in π_*^s and spherical classes in H_*Q_0S^0*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.06752) (arXiv:1504.06752, 2015). Gives partial results toward Curtis's conjecture, including non-decomposability of certain elements with nonzero Hurewicz image and the passage of the conjecture from a set to the ideal it generates, in the setting of Theorem 1.1.
- [Hadi Zare, *Generalised geometric weak conjecture on spherical classes and non-factorisation of Kervaire invariant one elements*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.02040) (arXiv:1512.02040, 2015). Shows that the Hurewicz image on products of positive-degree elements is determined by η², ν², σ², and proves Curtis's conjecture on the image of the n-fold transfer for n > 1; both are special cases of Theorem 1.1.
- [Hadi Zare, *Freudenthal theorem and spherical classes in $H_*QS^0$*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.06427) (arXiv:1801.06427, 2018-01-18; revised 2018-01-22). Its Hurewicz-vanishing results for stable classes factoring through certain finite spectra and its spherical-class calculations for certain finite loop spaces of spheres give prior partial results relevant to Theorem 1.1 and the Eccles consequence in Corollary 1.2.
- [Hadi Zare, *Filtered finiteness of the image of the unstable Hurewicz homomorphism with applications to bordism of immersions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.00735) (arXiv:1801.00735, 2018-01-02; revised 2018-01-03). Notes that finiteness of the set of spherical classes in H_*Q_0S^0 follows from Curtis's conjecture and proves a filtered finiteness result for Hurewicz images from finite loop spaces, for comparison with Corollary 1.3.
