## Possibly relevant literature (found by the agent, not checked)
- [Q. Yin, *Cycles on curves and Jacobians: a tale of two tautological rings*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.2216) (arXiv:1407.2216, 2014). Gets tautological relations from the motivic Lefschetz decomposition on the Jacobian side and asks whether all tautological relations are "motivic". This bears on the paper's route to Chow vanishing through O'Sullivan's motivic tensor decomposition of a curve's correspondences.
- [D. Petersen, M. Tavakol, Q. Yin, *Tautological classes with twisted coefficients*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08875) (arXiv:1705.08875, 2017). Studies tautological classes on all fibered powers $C_g^n$ at once through $Sp(2g)$-twisted coefficients. This may relate to the paper's odd symplectic summand and its color-preserving alternation on rational-tails strata. The paper cites Tavakol's $C_g^n$ vs. $M^{rt}_{g,n}$ comparison but not this work.
- [B. Moonen, Q. Yin, *Some remarks on modified diagonals*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.1646) (arXiv:1405.1646, 2014). Gives basic and sharp vanishing results for modified diagonal classes of curves. This may relate to the paper's "additional nilpotence identity" for the Gross–Schoen-type cubic $\varphi$.
- [D. Genlik, F. Janda, *From 3-spin to negative r-spin: limits of cohomological field theories and tautological relations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36509) (arXiv:2609.36509, Sept. 29, 2026). Proves that the negative r-spin relations (and hence the Kazarian–Norbury K-relations) are consequences of Pixton's 3-spin relations. This bears on the paper's discussion of which known relation systems lie in the span $\mathcal P_{g,n}$.
- [D. Petersen, O. Tommasi, *The Gorenstein conjecture fails for the tautological ring of $\overline{\mathcal M}_{2,n}$*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.5761) (arXiv:1210.5761, 2012). Gives the first Gorenstein failure on the full compactification. This may be useful context for the paper's discussion of completeness vs. Gorenstein; the paper cites Canning, Pixton and Canning–Larson–Schmitt for that discussion, not Petersen–Tommasi.
