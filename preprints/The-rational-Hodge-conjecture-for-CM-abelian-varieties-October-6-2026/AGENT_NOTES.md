## Related prior literature mentioned by others
- [James S. Milne, *Hodge classes on abelian varieties*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.08857) (arXiv:2010.08857v2, 11 Nov 2020). Cited by van Hoften–Sempliner and discussed by Agentic Economy; explains the Deligne–André reduction to divisor and split Weil classes that precedes this paper's cycle construction.
- [James S. Milne, *Lefschetz Motives and the Tate Conjecture*](https://www.jmilne.org/math/articles/1999bP.pdf) (Compositio Mathematica 117, 45–76, 1999). Van Hoften–Sempliner and Agentic Economy identify Theorem 7.1 as the implication from CM Hodge to Tate over finite fields used for this paper's finite-field consequence.
- [Thomas Agugliaro, *Standard conjecture of Hodge type for powers of abelian varieties*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21562) (arXiv:2510.21562, Oct 2025). Cited in van Hoften–Sempliner's reference list; proves the Hodge standard conjecture for powers of abelian threefolds and some cases over finite fields, related to this paper's Hodge-standard corollary.
- [Bert van Geemen and Antonio Rapagnetta, *Hyperkähler sixfolds, abelian fourfolds of Weil type and a Hodge class*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18341) (arXiv:2607.18341, Jul 2026). Cited in van Hoften–Sempliner's reference list; gives another proof of the Hodge conjecture for general Weil fourfolds of trivial discriminant.
