- [N. Frantzikinakis, *Some open problems on multiple ergodic averages*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.3808) (arXiv:1103.3808, v1 March 2011, v3 October 2016). Problem 11 (Section 5.1.3) asks about pointwise convergence of the averages with iterates $T^n, T^{2n}, T^{3n}$, noting it is "open even for weak mixing systems"; Theorem 1.1 addresses this question for mixing $T$ and every length.
- [V. V. Ryzhikov, *Multiple mixing, 75 years of Rokhlin's problem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07234) (arXiv:2411.07234, November 2024). A survey of Rokhlin's question whether mixing implies $k$-fold mixing; the paper uses a positive answer to that question as an input, citing the companion manuscript on Rokhlin's multiple-mixing problem.
- [Rene Rühr and Ronggang Shi, *Quantitative Multiple pointwise convergence and effective multiple correlations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.13055) (arXiv:1904.13055, 30 April 2019). Derives quantitative multiple pointwise ergodic theorems from effective higher-order correlations, a comparison point for this manuscript's convergence at every finite consecutive length without a mixing rate.

A recent survey of norm and pointwise questions for multiple ergodic averages, including open problems, giving background on norm versus almost-everywhere convergence; the fourfold companion cites it, but this paper does not.
