Assuming the single-fold conjecture that the paper's Theorem 1.1 asserts, it obtains for every computable $f$ and sufficiently large $n$ an $n$-variable system of elementary arithmetic equations with exactly $f(n)$ nonnegative integer solutions.
- [D. Cantone, L. Cuzziol, E. G. Omodeo, *Six equations in search of a finite-fold-ness proof*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.02208) (arXiv:2303.02208, v3 30 Sep 2024). Gives six quaternary quartic equations and shows that finite-foldness of any one of them would give finite-fold representations of all r.e. sets, an alternative conditional route to the finite-fold conjecture that the paper derives from Theorem 1.1.
