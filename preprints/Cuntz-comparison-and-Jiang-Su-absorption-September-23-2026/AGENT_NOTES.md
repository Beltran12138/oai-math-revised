- [H. Thiel, *Ranks of operators and pureness of C\*-algebras*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06761) (arXiv:2610.06761, 5 October 2026). Shows that for unital simple non-elementary algebras, strict comparison by traces gives almost divisibility and pureness without assuming exactness, using trace-preserving compression and rank-rounding lemmas; this overlaps with the paper's simple divisibility theorem (`ext:thm:divisibility`), and Thiel's paper does not cite the OpenAI papers.
- [S. Evington, S. Hua, C. Schafhauser, A. Seth, A. Tikuisis, S. White, *Regular simple nuclear C\*-algebras*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09390) (arXiv:2610.09390, 7 October 2026). Proves $\mathcal Z$-stability for all simple separable pure nuclear algebras (Theorem A, no unit assumed), which together with Thiel's work gives an independent route to the comparison-to-$\mathcal Z$-stability implication in Theorem 1.1. It does not cite the OpenAI manuscripts and says one lemma was first obtained with ChatGPT Astra.
- [J. Castillejos, S. Evington, *Stabilising uniform property Gamma*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.05792) (arXiv:2010.05792, 2020; Proc. Amer. Math. Soc.). Proves $\mathcal Z$-stability for simple separable nuclear algebras with stabilised property Gamma and $\mathrm{Cu}(A)\cong\mathrm{Cu}(A\otimes\mathcal Z)$, an earlier simple-case relative of the paper's Cuntz-semigroup criteria (`cor:canonical`, `cor:abstract-cu`) that the paper does not cite.
