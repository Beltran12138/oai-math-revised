Problem 11 (Section 5.1.3) asks about pointwise convergence of the averages with iterates $T^n, T^{2n}, T^{3n}$, noting it is "open even for weak mixing systems"; the main theorem treats the mixing case for arbitrary distinct nonzero integer slopes.
