- [Lu Wang, *Uniqueness of Self-similar Shrinkers with Asymptotically Conical Ends*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.0450) (arXiv:1110.0450, October 2011). Proves uniqueness of smooth properly embedded self-shrinking ends asymptotic to a given regular cone; relevant to the paper's stationary analysis of shrinkers with conical ends (only Wang's later end-structure paper, arXiv:1610.04904, is in the bibliography).
- [Lu Wang, *Uniqueness of self-similar shrinkers with asymptotically cylindrical ends*](https://portal.mardi4nfdi.de/wiki/Item:Q294271) (J. reine angew. Math., 2016, DOI 10.1515/CRELLE-2014-0006). Proves uniqueness of embedded shrinkers asymptotic to a generalized cylinder to infinite order, and constructs non-rotationally symmetric ends approaching cylinders at polynomial rates; relevant to the paper's remark that a shrinker with cylindrical ends need not be an exact round cylinder.
- [Tang-Kai Lee and Xinrui Zhao, *Uniqueness of conical singularities for mean curvature flows*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.10883) (arXiv:2301.10883, January 2023). Extends the Chodosh–Schulze uniqueness of asymptotically conical tangent flows, which the introduction cites as a preceding uniqueness class, to all codimensions.
- [Tobias Holck Colding, Tom Ilmanen and William P. Minicozzi II, *Rigidity of generic singularities of mean curvature flow*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.6356) (arXiv:1304.6356, April 2013; prepared for Publ. IHES). Proves rigidity of round cylinders among shrinkers; relevant because the paper says its result is obtained "without using cylinder rigidity", and the cited Bernstein–Wang note builds on this work.
