## Follow-up works

- [Jacob Sussman, *The Walsh-Hadamard transform with fewer operations*](https://github.com/jacobalansussman/wht-fewer-operations) (GitHub repository with Lean proofs, programs and a write-up, revision 2, October 2026). The author reports Lean proofs, in the paper's circuit model over $\mathbb C$, that the exact (unscaled) Walsh–Hadamard transform on $N = 8^j$ points has a circuit with at most $(5/6) N \log_2 N + N$ gates, and, in a variant over any commutative ring that charges one gate per line $\pm u \pm c v$ (constant $c$) as in Coppersmith, Feig and Linzer (1994), at most $(3/4) N \log_2 N + N/4$ for $N = 2^k \ge 4$. These improve leading constants only, which the paper says do not settle its question; the author says the circuits, proofs and write-up were produced by AI agents under his direction and have not yet been reviewed line by line by a person.
