- [Sung Rak Choi, Chuyu Zhou, *On existence of bounded relative-global complements for Fano fibrations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12031) (arXiv:2402.12031, 19 February 2024). Proves bounded relative-global complements for fixed-dimensional ε-lc Fano fibrations and klt complements over curves, next to the curve-base results (Birkar) that the paper surveys before Theorem 1.
- [Florin Ambro, *An explicit bound for complements on toric varieties*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18509) (arXiv:2506.18509, June 2025). Gives an effective bound for klt complement indices on toric Fano varieties; the paper's toric discussion cites only Ambro's earlier arXiv:2212.13862, and its τ(d, ε) is not effective.
- [Caucher Birkar, *Boundedness of ε-log Canonical Complements on Surfaces*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0409254) (arXiv:math/0409254, September 2004). Proves Shokurov's ε-lc complement boundedness on surfaces, related to the stronger expectation from Shokurov's 2004 formulation cited in the introduction.
- [Stefano Filipazzi and Joaquín Moraga, *Strong (δ, n)-Complements for Semi-Stable Morphisms*](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/26570) (Documenta Mathematica 25, 1953–1996, 2020). Theorem 1.4 gives local strong complements with a positive discrepancy bound under semistability, finite boundary coefficients and bounded Cartier index of the base canonical divisor, a restricted-setting comparison for bounded klt complements.
