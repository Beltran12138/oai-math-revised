- [Maciej Borodzik and Stefan Friedl, *A note on a topological approach to the μ-constant problem in dimension 2*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.1363) (arXiv:1304.1363, April 2013; [Revista Matemática Complutense 27, 345–350, 2014](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13163-013-0122-6)). Shows that certain homological and homotopical properties of cobordisms between arbitrary graph manifolds do not suffice to settle the dimension-2 case, while emphasizing the additional negative-definiteness condition satisfied by singularity links; this bears on the introduction's statement that the classical cobordism argument does not apply to surfaces.
- [Maximiliano Leyton-Álvarez, Hussein Mourtada and Mark Spivakovsky, *Newton non-degenerate μ-constant deformations admit simultaneous embedded resolutions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.10316) (arXiv:2001.10316; Compositio Math. 158 (2022), 1268–1297). Shows that a Newton non-degenerate deformation is μ-constant if and only if it admits a simultaneous embedded resolution, which gives topological triviality; this is a special case of Theorem 1.1, also proved through simultaneous resolution.
- [R. Giménez Conejero, Andreas Lind and Aurélio Menegon, *Embedded topological triviality of separable families of singularities*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23185) (arXiv:2511.23185, November 2025). Gives sufficient conditions for embedded topological triviality of families of function germs, with applications to a broad class of μ-constant deformations; this overlaps in part with the conclusion of Theorem 1.1.
- [Aurelio Menegon Neto, *On the topology of families of isolated singularities*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.7609) (arXiv:1411.7609, November 2014; withdrawn in v2). Claimed a solution of the μ-constant problem in all dimensions via uniform Milnor radii; the author withdrew it "due to a crucial error". This bears on the uniform-radius approach that the paper contrasts with its own when discussing Peters (2026).
