Reduces the logarithmic subadditivity conjecture to a special case of generalized abundance and proves it for affine varieties; relevant to the paper's logarithmic subadditivity corollary, and not in its bibliography.
- [Bojie He and Xiangyu Zhou, *On the subadditivity of generalized Kodaira dimensions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.08265) (arXiv:2405.08265, May 2024). Gives an analytic proof of Fujino's subadditivity result for log Kodaira dimensions via multiplier-ideal versions of numerical Kodaira dimension; an analytic approach to the same logarithmic inequality.
