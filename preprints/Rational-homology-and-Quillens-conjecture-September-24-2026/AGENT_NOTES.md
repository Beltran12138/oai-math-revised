## Possibly relevant literature (found by the agent, not checked)
- [Kevin I. Piterman, Iván Sadofschi Costa, Antonio Viruel, *Acyclic 2-dimensional complexes and Quillen's conjecture*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.02141) (arXiv:1907.02141, 2019; rev. 2020). Proves Quillen's conjecture for groups of p-rank 3 via G-invariant acyclic 2-dimensional complexes, including cases outside Aschbacher–Smith; this known case is not covered by the manuscript's account of prior results or its bibliography.
- [Kevin Iván Piterman, *Spherical p-group complexes arising from finite groups of Lie type*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07489) (arXiv:2403.07489, 2024). Studies p-group complexes of groups with a self-centralising Lie-type normal subgroup in characteristic p and their nonzero Euler characteristic, which bears on the defining-characteristic components the manuscript handles via Piterman (arXiv:2607.27500).
- [Kevin Ivan Piterman, *On the frame complex of symplectic spaces*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02940) (arXiv:2305.02940, 2023). Computes homology vanishing for the frame complex of a symplectic space over a finite field, which is related to the symplectic frame cycles constructed in the manuscript's Section 7.
- [Kevin Ivan Piterman, John Shareshian, Volkmar Welker, *Posets of decompositions in spherical buildings*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.22531) (arXiv:2511.22531, 2025). Studies decomposition and common-bases posets in spherical buildings, related to the manuscript's frame/apartment-chain construction, which cites only the unitary case (Piterman–Welker 2024).
- [Elias Gabriel Minian, Kevin Ivan Piterman, *The fundamental group of the p-subgroup complex*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.03549) (arXiv:1903.03549, 2019). Studies the homotopy type (fundamental group) of the same p-subgroup complexes, giving background on their topology beyond rational homology.
