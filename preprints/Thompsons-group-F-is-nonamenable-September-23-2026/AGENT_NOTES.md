- [Sang-hyun Kim and Yash Lodha, *A note on the proof of nonamenability of F*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18360) (arXiv:2610.18360, 13 Oct 2026). Describes itself as "a human written exposition of OpenAI's recent proof that Thompson's group F is nonamenable"; it gives a streamlined argument by showing the action of F on the dyadic rationals is not extensively amenable, and states a generalization (Theorem 2.7) to groups of permutations of a set, with its proof omitted.
- [Koji Fujiwara, *Thompson's group F is not weakly amenable*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27932) (arXiv:2610.27932, 20 Oct 2026). Embeds the semidirect product of Z^(D) (D the dyadic rationals in [0,1]) by F into F and deduces non-weak-amenability from nonamenability of F, citing this paper as Theorem 1.1 and the Kim–Lodha note for a streamlined proof.
- [Chris Bruce and Shirly Geffen, *C\*-selflessness and Z-stability for Thompson's group F*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28473) (arXiv:2610.28473, 20 Oct 2026). Proves that the reduced C\*-algebra of F is Z-stable and selfless, using this paper's nonamenability theorem together with another release preprint (*The Kirchberg–Rørdam character criterion*).
