Gives a simple example in which the nodal Hausdorff measure of sub-Laplacian eigenfunctions is not bounded by $C\sqrt\lambda$, an earlier failure of the $\sqrt\lambda$ upper bound in a hypoelliptic, non-Riemannian setting; not cited in the paper.
