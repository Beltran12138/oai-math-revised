Gives another proof of the polynomial lower bounds at p=1, including the near-linear bound of Andoni–Charikar–Neiman–Nguyen for distortion 1+ε; this concerns the endpoint obstruction the paper cites via Brinkman–Charikar and Lee–Naor.

Builds nonlinear dimension-reduction embeddings for finite subsets of ℓp with 1≤p≤2, with bounds in terms of intrinsic dimension; compare the paper's survey of earlier finite-set bounds below p=2 (Schechtman).
