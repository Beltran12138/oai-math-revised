It shows that the canonical bundle of a "Kähler exact" manifold of general type is ample, which is close to the Gromov/Chen–Yang Kähler-hyperbolic line discussed in the introduction.
- [Xueyuan Wan, *Positivity of the canonical bundle and Hermitian metrics with quasi-negative holomorphic sectional curvature*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08111) (arXiv:2610.08111, 6 Oct 2026). It proves $K_X$ ample for compact Kähler manifolds carrying a Hermitian metric of quasi-negative Chern holomorphic sectional curvature, a criterion parallel to the Wu–Yau, Diverio–Trapani and Broder–Stanfield results surveyed in the introduction.
