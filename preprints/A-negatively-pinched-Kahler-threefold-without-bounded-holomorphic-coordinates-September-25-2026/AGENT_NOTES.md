## Possibly relevant literature (found by the agent, not checked)
- [F. Bracci, H. Gaussier, A. Zimmer, *The geometry of domains with negatively pinched Kähler metrics*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.11389) (arXiv:1810.11389, 2018, revised 2022). Studies how a complete Kähler metric with pinched negative holomorphic bisectional curvature on a domain in C^d constrains its boundary, relevant to the paper's construction of a domain M ⊂ C^3 carrying such a metric.
- [J. Cao, M.-C. Shaw, *Bounded holomorphic functions on negatively curved Kähler manifolds of dimension ≥ 3*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00368) (arXiv:1512.00368, 2015, withdrawn 2016). This withdrawn preprint announced nonconstant bounded holomorphic functions under two-sided negative pinching in complex dimension at least 3, the setting in which the paper notes its example has two nonconstant bounded base-coordinate functions; it is cited in the companion paper but not in this paper's bibliography.
- [Nikolay Shcherbina and Liyou Zhang, *A Kobayashi and Bergman complete domain without bounded representations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.06728) (arXiv:2003.06728, 2020-03-15). Their strictly pseudoconvex domain in complex dimension two has complete Kobayashi and Bergman metrics and no nonconstant bounded holomorphic functions, a comparison point for the paper's distinction between intrinsic-metric completeness and bounded holomorphic coordinates.
