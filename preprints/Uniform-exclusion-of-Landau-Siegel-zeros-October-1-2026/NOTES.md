## Follow-up works

- [William D. Banks, *On the OpenAI proof that Landau-Siegel zeros do not exist*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15483) (arXiv:2610.15483, 2026-10-11). A detailed proof of this paper's argument with explicit constants, covering all conductors in combination with [Lu–Zaman–Zhao](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03626).
- [Bryce Kerr, *A simpler proof that there are no Landau--Siegel zeros*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21099) (arXiv:2610.21099, 2026-10-15). Replaces the interpolation and determinant analysis with a pigeonhole polynomial and Masser's zero estimate.
- [Ben Green, *On Landau--Siegel zeros*](https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00311) (hexagon:2610.00311, 2026-10-17). A variant working in the quadratic field with an explicit Gram determinant, avoiding the biquadratic field.
- [Terence Tao, *A sketch of the proof of non-existence of Siegel zeroes*](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/10/19/a-sketch-of-the-proof-of-non-existence-of-siegel-zeroes/) (What's new, 2026-10-19). An expository sketch of Green's argument.
- [Kevin Barreto, *An elementary proof of the non-existence of Landau–Siegel zeros*](https://x.com/acerfur/status/2112239827077026128) (posted on X, 2026-10-19). A three-page variant of Green's argument using a Wronskian in place of resultants, with a sharper bound.
- Jacob Pescador (private communication, [mentioned by Ben Green](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/10/19/a-sketch-of-the-proof-of-non-existence-of-siegel-zeroes/#comment-698405), 2026-10-19). A four-page variant of the proof.
- [Roelof van Dijk, *A Frobenius determinant proof of the non-existence of Landau–Siegel zeros*](https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00409) (hexagon:2610.00409, 2026-10-20). Uses the Frobenius congruence for both divisibility and nonvanishing of the determinant, avoiding resultants and Wronskians.
- [Victor Zhenyu Guo, *An explicit elementary proof of no Landau--Siegel zeros*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30476) (arXiv:2610.30476, 2026-10-21). An elementary proof with the explicit constant c = 1/100 for every conductor q ≥ 3.
- [Scott Kominers, *Small algebraic integers and Frobenius prime sums*](https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00451) (hexagon:2610.00451, 2026-10-21). Applies the determinant argument to every Frobenius class in a finite Galois extension of Q, giving a weighted Chebotarev-type bound.
- [William D. Banks, *Landau-Siegel zeros over a base field and effective Brauer-Siegel*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31402) (arXiv:2610.31402, 2026-10-22). Extends the exclusion to quadratic extensions of a fixed number field and deduces an effective Brauer–Siegel theorem.
- [Paul Nelson, *Some notes on recent results on Landau–Siegel zeros*](https://ultronozm.github.io/math/20261020T163023--siegel-zeros.html) (online notes, 2026-10-20). A detailed account of the method for Frobenius classes in Galois extensions of number fields, with explicit constants.
- [Paul Pollack, *The missing Lin(ni)k*](https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00545) (hexagon:2610.00545, 2026-10-26). Combines the exclusion of Landau–Siegel zeros with Selberg's symmetry formulas to give an elementary proof of Linnik's theorem.
